# calo App Icon Design

## Concept
A minimalist, modern icon representing calorie tracking and healthy eating.

## Design Choice: "Fork & Plate with 'c'"
- **Primary Element**: Stylized lowercase "c" forming a plate outline
- **Secondary Element**: Fork integrated into the design
- **Color Scheme**: 
  - Primary: Fresh green (#4CAF50) - represents health, nutrition
  - Accent: Orange (#FF9800) - represents energy, calories
  - Background: White/Light gradient

## Icon Sizes Needed (iOS & macOS)
- 1024x1024 (App Store)
- 180x180 (iPhone @3x)
- 120x120 (iPhone @2x)
- 167x167 (iPad Pro @2x)  
- 152x152 (iPad @2x)
- 128x128 (macOS)
- 64x64 (macOS)
- 32x32 (macOS)
- 16x16 (macOS)

## SF Symbol Alternative
For development, we'll use SF Symbol: "fork.knife.circle.fill" with custom coloring

## Implementation
1. Use SF Symbol for development
2. Generate proper app icon using icon generator service
3. Place in Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/
